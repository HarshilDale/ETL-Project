 In this project, I extracted retail data using Kaggle APIs and built a complete data pipeline to analyze business performance and trends. Here’s a quick breakdown of the key steps and insights:

🔹 Key Steps
 1️. Data Extraction: Utilizing Kaggle APIs to fetch retail datasets.
 2️. Data Preprocessing: Cleaning and transforming raw data using Pandas to ensure quality and consistency.
 3️. Data Loading: Importing the processed data into SQL Server for further operations.
 4️. Analytics & Insights: Running SQL queries to generate insights for business decision-making.

🔍 Analytical Highlights
 • Top 10 Revenue-Generating Products – A query identifies the products that generate the most sales by summing their sale prices.
 • Top 5 Best-Selling Products by Region – A Common Table Expression (CTE) is used to rank and find the top 5 products by sales in each region.
 • Month-over-Month Sales Growth (2022 vs. 2023) – A CTE compares sales growth for each month between the two years.
 • Highest-Selling Category by Month – Another CTE finds the highest-selling month for each category.
• Highest Profit Growth Sub-Category (2023 vs. 2022) – he query calculates the sub-category with the largest increase in profit between 2022 and 2023.

These SQL operations provide key business insights, such as top-selling products, regional sales performance, sales trends, and profit growth, facilitating better decision-making.
